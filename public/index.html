    • 1 On the left side of the website URL at the top of Chrome, click the Lock (🔒) / Settings icon.
    • 2 Permissions (Permissions) -> Notifications Select it.
    • 3 Allow (ON) Turn it on.
    • 4 Also make sure Phone Settings -> Apps -> Chrome -> Notifications is ON.
    • 1 In Safari, first use "Add to Phone" to add this app to your phone home screen.
    • 2 Open the iPhone's main Settings and open Notifications or Safari.
    • 3 Allow Notifications and turn it ON.
    • 1 In the browser address bar, click the Tune / Lock (🔒) icon.
    • 2 Turn the switch next to Notifications to Allow (ON).
